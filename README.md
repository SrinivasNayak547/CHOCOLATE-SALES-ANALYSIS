# 🍫 Chocolate Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

The Chocolate Sales Analysis project is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze chocolate sales performance, revenue generation, product demand, and regional sales distribution. It provides valuable business insights through interactive visualizations and key performance indicators (KPIs).

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall chocolate sales performance.
- Track total revenue and sales quantity.
- Identify top-performing chocolate products.
- Analyze sales performance by country.
- Evaluate salesperson performance.
- Monitor monthly and yearly sales trends.
- Identify high-revenue products and regions.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Power BI** – Dashboard development and visualization.
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation.
- **DAX** – Calculated measures and KPIs.
- **Excel** – Dataset preparation and analysis.

## 📊 Dashboard Features

- Total Sales Revenue
- Total Boxes Sold
- Total Shipments
- Average Sales per Shipment
- Product-wise Sales Analysis
- Country-wise Sales Performance
- Salesperson Performance Analysis
- Monthly Sales Trends
- Top 10 Products Analysis
- Sales Growth Analysis

## 📈 Key Insights

- Identified top-performing chocolate products.
- Analyzed country-wise revenue contribution.
- Evaluated individual salesperson performance.
- Identified monthly sales trends.
- Compared product demand and sales performance.
- Analyzed revenue distribution across different regions.
